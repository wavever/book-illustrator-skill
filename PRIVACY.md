# Privacy

The canonical privacy, data-flow and source-terms notice ships with the skill:
[`skills/book-illustrator/PRIVACY.md`](skills/book-illustrator/PRIVACY.md).

In short, the scripts do not upload the full book, its paragraphs or working files, and they send no
telemetry. Entity search names from the book go to the image sources (Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons,
Openverse, and legacy sources only if you opt in). Depending on the host agent and model, the
chapter text the agent reads may be sent to a model provider.
